# Willow-to-Cadence adaptation matrix

| Willow pattern | User problem solved | Cadence-native adaptation | Constraints | Do not copy | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| One-question onboarding rhythm | New users understand why each choice matters. | Versioned onboarding controller with focused screens, Back, Skip where learning is optional, and replay from Settings. | Never reset meetings, recordings, Keychain, or permissions; no role/acquisition/workspace collection. | Gradient treatment, wording, role/source questions. | Approved onboarding map with fresh Cadence frames for new, skipped, resumed, denied, and replayed states. |
| Privacy choice before practice | The data boundary is explicit before voice use. | Local-first default with a concise per-request Scribe egress disclosure. | Privacy mode must hard-disable network provider work; Screen Recording stays meeting-only. | Training claims without a real technical contract. | Settings/onboarding state matrix plus privacy tests. |
| Separate Scribe and Dictation lessons and shortcuts | Users can choose literal voice input or assisted writing intentionally. | Separate Dictation/Scribe actions, independent shortcuts, and a mode-first first-use explanation. | Dictation latency path and meeting pipeline stay independent. | Shortcut keys, demo copy, mode labels where they conflict with Cadence vocabulary. | Intent-picker and shortcut prototype. |
| Practice in a fake familiar app | Users learn without risking real content. | Synthetic rehearsal targets and non-persistent mock provider states. | No audio, transcript, selected text, or generated result survives rehearsal. | Willow's fake-app visual treatment or copy. | Rehearsal privacy proof and accessibility review. |
| Shortcut recovery editor | A failed hotkey has an immediate recovery route. | Inline shortcut validation with explicit conflict, denied-permission, and retry outcomes. | Must not steal target-app focus or leave global handlers ambiguous. | Color-only success signal. | Keyboard/VoiceOver/focus prototype evidence. |
| Hold-specific lock on the compact HUD | Users can distinguish a latched listening mode from a transient non-hold interaction at a glance. | Show a distinct, accessible latch indicator only while Cadence is genuinely in its corresponding persistent capture state; describe that state in VoiceOver and never imply privacy guarantees. | Must derive from the coordinator state, remain readable with reduced motion, and disappear immediately when the latch ends. | Willow's glyph treatment, color, placement, or undocumented gesture contract. | Cadence hold/non-hold/processing/error captures plus accessibility labels and state-transition tests. |
| Context-specific style presets | People understand style as a practical output choice. | Cadence Style Profiles scoped by app/context, with tone/length/format examples. | Local persistence, deterministic fallback, no analytics of profile content. | Willow example messages and exact preset language. | Profile IA and populated/empty/error state matrix. |
| Dictionary tabs plus explicit add | Language customization is discoverable without being central to recording. | Personal Terms and Shortcuts as a dedicated Cadence destination with clear add/edit/disable/test actions. | Never log or transmit terms/templates. | Existing Willow terms, shortcuts, fields, or source. | Synthetic populated and empty captures plus persistence tests. |
| Home learning checklist | Returning users can discover the next safe capability. | Quiet first-use checklist on Cadence's appropriate workspace surface; hide or collapse after completion. | Must not compete with meeting capture or pretend a workflow is complete. | Growth metrics, referral incentives, team upsells. | Navigation map and first-run/returning-user prototype. |
| Grouped settings plus search | Advanced controls remain findable without crowding core use. | Settings categories for Dictation, Scribe readiness, privacy, language/model, onboarding replay, and integrations. | Native macOS menu/settings behavior; destructive actions separated. | Willow sidebar geometry, copy, and visual identity. | Scrolled, narrow, VoiceOver, high-contrast, and reduced-transparency states. |

## Non-negotiable Cadence deltas

- The compact HUD remains a truthful projection of a real coordinator state. Its review surface cannot promise keyboard access while also leaving focus behavior undefined.
- Meeting capture durability, transcript lineage, recovery, and privacy boundaries remain untouched.
- No ambient screen capture, always-on listening, referrals, invitations, team-growth flow, or copied proprietary media belongs in the resulting product.
